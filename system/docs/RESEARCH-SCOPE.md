**Title.** OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOUR, UNCERTAIN INFERENCE: A CALIBRATED, HUMAN-GOVERNED CNN FRAMEWORK FOR ASSESSING PRE-SERVICE TEACHERS DURING TEACHING PRACTICUM IN GHANAIAN BASIC SCHOOLS

## Research questions

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| RQ1 | What requirements must a convolutional teaching-assessment artifact satisfy to be accepted as valid, usable, and governable by Ghanaian teacher educators, and does an architecture meeting them pass expert design validation? |
| RQ2 | With what per-class accuracy, calibration, and reliability can a teacher-independent spatiotemporal model detect the five observable behaviours in teaching-practicum classrooms, relative to agreement among trained human raters? |
| RQ3 | How does model performance and calibration change across held-out practicum schools and colleges, which measured classroom conditions are associated with degradation, and can out-of-distribution detection flag unfamiliar conditions? |
| RQ4 | Where does the boundary fall between behaviours a calibrated model recovers reliably and judgments that resist automation, and what does the resulting placement imply for the National Teachers’ Standards domains each behaviour informs? |
| RQ5 | Does uncertainty-triggered routing to human adjudication produce appropriate reliance, measured behaviourally through seeded-error probes, and what is its effect on review time, score consistency, and workload? |
| RQ6 | What transferable design principles for human-governed machine perception in high-stakes educational assessment follow, and how do infrastructure and governance constraints condition them? |

## Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Human inter-rater agreement differs systematically across the five behaviours, highest for spatial and gestural measures and lowest for postural stance. | Tests the premise of the decomposition |
| H2 | On held-out teachers from the development schools, model-to-panel agreement is non-inferior to single-rater-to-panel agreement for at least three of the five behaviours. | Non-inferiority, margin fixed in advance |
| H3 | Performance varies across placement schools and colleges, with the largest predicted degradation in spatial mobility and the smallest in gesture production. | Predicted negative; publishing it is part of the contribution |
| H4 | Calibration deteriorates more than accuracy under shifts in practicum recording and classroom conditions, producing overconfidence in some held-out settings. | The central technical prediction |
| H5 | Out-of-distribution detection flags unfamiliar held-out classroom conditions at a higher rate than development-domain test sessions, at a stated false-positive cost. | Tests the safety mechanism |
| H6 | Uncertainty-triggered routing increases detection of seeded errors relative to an always-on display of model output. | Tests whether governance works |
| H7 | Evaluator review time falls against full-video manual review and does not rise materially against live observation. | Two-sided |

## Specific objectives

| # | Objective |
|---|---|
| O1 | Elicit and expert-validate the requirements and architecture for a calibrated, explainable, human-governed teaching-assessment artifact, using at least eight teacher-education and computer-science specialists. |
| O2 | Establish human inter-rater agreement per behaviour and construct a leakage-resistant annotated corpus of authentic basic-school teaching practicum across participating colleges. |
| O3 | Develop a teacher-independent spatiotemporal convolutional model for the five observable behaviours, reporting calibration and per-class performance against the human agreement band. |
| O4 | Characterise performance variation across held-out placement schools and colleges, examine its association with classroom conditions, and evaluate out-of-distribution detection. |
| O5 | Implement the explanation, uncertainty-routing, adjudication, audit-trail, and monitoring components as a deployable, on-premise-capable artifact across at least two Colleges of Education. |
| O6 | Evaluate reliance, review time, score consistency, and workload with teacher educators, and derive transferable design principles. |

## Target behaviours

| # | Behaviour | Observable definition | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Gesture production | Rate and amplitude of hand and arm movement above a motion threshold. | Retained |
| B2 | Body orientation to class | Head and torso yaw relative to the learner region. | Revised from eye contact: gaze estimation at classroom distance is unreliable, head pose is mature |
| B3 | Spatial position and mobility | Teacher location, zone dwell time, and proximity transitions. | Retained; carries the largest expected domain shift |
| B4 | Postural stance | Standing or seated, torso lean, open or closed stance. | Retained |
| B5 | Board and material use | Teacher at board; handling of an instructional artifact. | Replaces facial expressiveness |
